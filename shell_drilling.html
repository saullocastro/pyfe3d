Drilling rotations#

The rotation \(r_z\) about the normal of a shell element, the drilling rotation, appears in none of the strains of the FSDT, see (3). Without a term of its own, its rows and columns in the element matrix are empty, and a mesh of coplanar elements gives a singular global stiffness matrix; when the elements are not coplanar, the drilling rotation of one element combines with the bending rotations of its neighbours. Shells connected to beams also need the drilling rotation to transmit in-plane moments. Every shell element of pyfe3d ties \(r_z\) to the in-plane rotation of the membrane field,

(1)#\[\theta_z = \frac{1}{2} \left( v_{,x} - u_{,y} \right)\]

with the operator

(2)#\[\boldsymbol{B}_{r_z} = \boldsymbol{S}^{r_z} + \frac{1}{2} \boldsymbol{S}^u_{,y} - \frac{1}{2} \boldsymbol{S}^v_{,x}, \qquad r_z - \theta_z = \boldsymbol{B}_{r_z} \boldsymbol{u}_e\]

the row BLdrilling of the probes, see Quad4Probe.update_BL(). Being built from \(r_z - \theta_z\), and not added on the diagonal, the term does not stiffen a rigid rotation of the element about its normal, such that all rigid-body motions and constant-strain states are represented exactly. Two models, selected with the attribute drilling_model of each element, give it a coefficient.

Fictitious penalty, drilling_model = 1#

The approach of MSC Nastran and Autodesk Nastran, through their parameter K6ROT, adds the penalty energy

(3)#\[U_{r_z} = \frac{1}{2} K6ROT \cdot 10^{-6} \int_A A_{66} \left( r_z - \theta_z \right)^2 dA\]

whose only purpose is to remove the singularity; the forces associated with it are spurious, and a moment about the normal recovered from the internal forces is meaningless. The quick reference guide of Autodesk Nastran recommends K6ROT = 100 for static analyses and \(10^4\) for modal analyses, whereas that of MSC Nastran states that values above 100 should not be used. The approach is similar to Eq. (2.20) of Adam et al. (2013).

Physics-based stiffness, drilling_model = 0#

The default replaces the penalty coefficient by the regularisation of Hughes and Brezzi (1989), with the coefficient \(\gamma_{r_z} = A_{66}\) of the laminate, \(G h\) for an isotropic plate, the value that they identify as natural for the isotropic problem. In Quad4 and Tria3R the in-plane field is, in addition, enriched with the drilling rotations after Allman (1984), in the combination of Ibrahimbegovic, Taylor and Wilson (1990) for the quadrilateral, such that the drilling rotation produces membrane strain energy. Each edge \(k\), joining the nodes \(i\) and \(j\), of length \(\ell_k\) and unit normal \(\boldsymbol{n}_k\), carries a quadratic normal displacement whose end slopes are identified with the drilling rotations of its nodes:

(4)#\[\begin{split}\begin{Bmatrix} u \\ v \end{Bmatrix} = \sum_i S_i \begin{Bmatrix} u_i \\ v_i \end{Bmatrix} + \sum_k \hat{S}_k \, \frac{\ell_k}{8} \left( {r_z}_i - {r_z}_j \right) \boldsymbol{n}_k\end{split}\]

The enrichment introduces no new degree of freedom: its amplitudes are the differences of the drilling rotations at the ends of each edge. The hierarchical bubble \(\hat{S}_k\) is 1 at the mid-point of the edge and 0 at every corner:

(5)#\[\text{quadrilateral:} \quad \hat{S}_k = \frac{1}{2} (1 - \xi^2)(1 + \eta_k \eta) \;\; (\eta = \eta_k = \pm 1), \quad \hat{S}_k = \frac{1}{2} (1 + \xi_k \xi)(1 - \eta^2) \;\; (\xi = \xi_k = \pm 1); \qquad \text{triangle:} \quad \hat{S}_k = 4 S_i S_j\]

the mid-side functions of the eight-node serendipity element for the quadrilateral, see the Notes of pyfe3d.quad4 and pyfe3d.tria3r. The enrichment fills the drilling columns of the membrane rows, \(\tilde{\boldsymbol{B}}_m\), and of the drilling row, \(\tilde{\boldsymbol{B}}_{r_z} = \boldsymbol{S}^{r_z} + \frac{1}{2} \tilde{\boldsymbol{S}}^u_{,y} - \frac{1}{2} \tilde{\boldsymbol{S}}^v_{,x}\), and adds to the stiffness matrix

(6)#\[\boldsymbol{K}_{C0_e}^{r_z} = \gamma_{r_z} \int_A \tilde{\boldsymbol{B}}_{r_z}^\top \tilde{\boldsymbol{B}}_{r_z} \, dA, \qquad \gamma_{r_z} = A_{66}\]

The curvature and transverse shear rows are not enriched. Allman’s enrichment alone is rank-deficient by one: \(u_i = v_i = 0\) with the same \({r_z}_i = \omega_0\) at every node makes every amplitude vanish and produces no strain, although it is not a rigid motion; the term (6) gives it the energy \(\frac{1}{2} \gamma_{r_z} A_e \omega_0^2\) and restores the rank of the element.

Two quadratures matter, following Ibrahimbegovic et al. (1990). The edge modes make the membrane strains vary linearly, and the membrane is integrated with three by three points in Quad4 and the Allman terms with three points in Tria3R: with two by two points the alternating pattern of the drilling rotations of the quadrilateral produces no membrane strain at any of the four points, a zero-energy mode that no value of \(\gamma_{r_z}\) removes. The term (6) is integrated with one point at the centroid: fully integrated, it over-constrains \(r_z = \theta_z\) and locks the membrane response as \(\gamma_{r_z}\) grows, whereas with one point the response reaches an asymptote, and one constraint per element is what is needed to remove the uniform drilling mode. The element response then has a broad plateau of insensitivity to \(\gamma_{r_z}\), and the attribute gamma_rz, negative by default to select \(A_{66}\), serves the sensitivity study that the literature recommends rather than normal use.

Unlike the penalty, the term is consistent: stationarity with respect to \(r_z\) gives \(\gamma_{r_z} (r_z - \theta_z) = 0\) pointwise, such that the exact solution has \(r_z = \theta_z\) and the term contributes no energy for any positive \(\gamma_{r_z}\). The nodal moments about the normal recovered from the internal forces are physical, and in-plane moments are transmitted between shells and beams through the shared drilling degree of freedom. With \(\boldsymbol{B} \ne \boldsymbol{0}\), for an offset or an unsymmetric laminate, the enriched membrane strains couple \(r_z\) to the curvatures. The enrichment also enters the membrane forces of the geometric stiffness and the non-linear terms, such that KC0 + KCNL + KG remains the exact Jacobian of the internal forces.

The default K6ROT is 100. for Quad4, Quad4R and Tria3R and 1. for Tria3DSG, see the attributes of each class. Quad4R has no enrichment because its one-point integration already removes the excessive in-plane bending stiffness that the enrichment removes in Quad4, see Quad4R: one-point integration and hourglass control, and Tria3DSG because it is aimed at the transverse shear, see Tria3DSG: the discrete shear gap triangle. The three-point rule of Tria3DSG is needed, since one point would constrain a single combination of its three drilling rotations.

Choosing a drilling model#

The physics-based model is the default because it is the correct one when the drilling moment is part of the load path, when shells are connected to beams or stiffeners that transmit in-plane moments, or when the mesh is too coarse for the unenriched membrane to be trusted. It is much more accurate in in-plane bending: on the skew membrane of Cook, reference 23.9, a two by two mesh of Quad4 gives 20.8 against 11.8 with the penalty, and a four by four mesh of split quadrilaterals of Tria3R gives 20.7 against 11.3. On coarse meshes of doubly curved shells the enrichment stiffens Tria3R, see Tria3R: discrete shear gap with the Allman membrane.
