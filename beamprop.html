Beam property module (pyfe3d.beamprop)#

Defines BeamProp, the cross-section and material properties read by the elements BeamC, BeamLR and Truss.

class pyfe3d.beamprop.BeamProp#

General class to represent beam properties.

No shear correction factors or geometric correction factors are applied internally during the calculation of the structural matrices; any correction must be applied by the user to the attributes.

The constructor takes no argument and sets all attributes to 0. The attributes are read, each time it is called, by the update_* methods of BeamC, BeamLR and Truss that receive the property.

The integrals over the cross section are taken with the coordinates \(y\) and \(z\) of the element coordinate system, measured from the beam axis that joins the two nodes of the element.

G12, G13 and GJ follow G until they are set: reading G12 or G13 returns G, and reading GJ returns G*J, with the values of G and J at the time of reading. Once set, the attribute keeps its own value, independently of G and J, until it is deleted, e.g. del prop.G12, which makes it follow G again. A property that sets only G and J gives the same matrices as in the versions of pyfe3d without G12, G13 and GJ.

Attributes:
A,float

Area of the cross section, dimension length squared.

E,float

Young modulus, force per unit area.

G,float

Shear modulus, force per unit area. It is the value of G12 and G13 when they are not set, and it multiplies J in GJ when GJ is not set.

G12,float

Transverse shear modulus of the element plane \(xy\), of the bending with the displacement \(v\), force per unit area. Returns G when not set.

G13,float

Transverse shear modulus of the element plane \(xz\), of the bending with the displacement \(w\), force per unit area. Returns G when not set.

GJ,float

Torsional stiffness, dimension force times length squared. Returns G*J when not set.

Iyy,float

Second moment of area about the y axis, the integral of the square of \(z\) over the cross section, dimension length to the fourth.

Izz,float

Second moment of area about the z axis, the integral of the square of \(y\) over the cross section, dimension length to the fourth.

Iyz,float

Product moment of area, the integral of \(y\) times \(z\) over the cross section, dimension length to the fourth.

J,float

Torsion constant, dimension length to the fourth. The elements use it only through GJ, when GJ is not set.

Ay,float

Integral of \(y\) over the cross section, dimension length cubed.

Az,float

Integral of \(z\) over the cross section, dimension length cubed.

intrho,float

Integral of the density over the cross section, dimension mass per unit length.

intrhoy,float

Integral of \(y\) times the density over the cross section, dimension mass.

intrhoz,float

Integral of \(z\) times the density over the cross section, dimension mass.

intrhoy2,float

Integral of the square of \(y\) times the density over the cross section, dimension mass times length.

intrhoz2,float

Integral of the square of \(z\) times the density over the cross section, dimension mass times length.

intrhoyz,float

Integral of \(y\) times \(z\) times the density over the cross section, dimension mass times length.

Notes

The attributes are the following integrals over the cross section, where \(\rho\) Is the density:

  • Iyy: \(\int_y \int_z z^2 dy dz\)

  • Izz: \(\int_y \int_z y^2 dy dz\)

  • Iyz: \(\int_y \int_z y z dy dz\)

  • Ay: \(\int_y \int_z y dy dz\)

  • Az: \(\int_y \int_z z dy dz\)

  • intrho: \(\int_{y_e} \int_{z_e} \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

  • intrhoy: \(\int_y \int_z y \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

  • intrhoz: \(\int_y \int_z z \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

  • intrhoy2: \(\int_y \int_z y^2 \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

  • intrhoz2: \(\int_y \int_z z^2 \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

  • intrhoyz: \(\int_y \int_z y z \rho(y, z) dy dz\)

In the beam elements, see BeamC, the transverse shear strains of the rigid cross section at the point \((y, z)\) are \(\gamma_{xy} - z {r_x}_{,x}\) and \(\gamma_{xz} + y {r_x}_{,x}\), where \(\gamma_{xy}\) and \(\gamma_{xz}\) are those of the beam axis and \({r_x}_{,x}\) is the twist. With \(G_{12}\) = G12 multiplying the first and \(G_{13}\) = G13 the second, their integral over the cross section gives the shear part of the strain energy per unit length, \(\frac{1}{2} \boldsymbol{\gamma}^\top \boldsymbol{D}_s \boldsymbol{\gamma}\) with \(\boldsymbol{\gamma} = \{\gamma_{xy}, \gamma_{xz}, {r_x}_{,x}\}^\top\) and

\[\begin{split}\boldsymbol{D}_s = \begin{bmatrix} G_{12} A & 0 & -G_{12} A_z \\ 0 & G_{13} A & G_{13} A_y \\ -G_{12} A_z & G_{13} A_y & G_{12} I_{yy} + G_{13} I_{zz} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

The elements replace the last entry, the torsional stiffness of the rigid cross section without warping, by GJ, given by the user, e.g. \(G J\) with the torsion constant \(J\) [1] of the Saint-Venant torsion, in which the cross section warps freely. G12 and G13 also enter the shear parameters \(\alpha_y\) and \(\alpha_z\) of the shape functions of BeamC. With non-zero Ay and Az, i.e. when the beam axis is not the centroidal axis, the axial strain is coupled with the bending curvatures and the transverse shear strains with the twist. intrhoy, intrhoz and intrhoyz couple the inertia of the translations and of the rotations, see BeamC.update_M().

The shear correction factors \(k_y\) and \(k_z\) of the two planes are applied to the moduli, G12 \(= k_y G\) and G13 \(= k_z G\), which leaves the torsional stiffness G*J unchanged. It is possible to have more complex scenarios where the different cross section parameters Ay, Az and GJ have different corrections applied due to geometric properties. It is the user’s responsability to apply these correction factors properly to achieve a general representation of the beam element behaviour by means of this property class.

For beams with homogeneous material along the cross section some of the quantities above can be calculated as follows, assuming that rho is the material density:

intrho = A*rho intrhoy2 = Izz*rho intrhoz2 = Iyy*rho intrhoyz = Iyz*rho
